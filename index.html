Good to know

Questions,
answered clearly.

Does HeatoMeter upload my health or screen-time data?+

No. Battery readings, temperature history, preferences, and Usage Access calculations remain on your device. Google services are used for consent-managed advertising and Play Billing.

Can it measure room temperature?+

Battery temperature is not ambient room temperature. If a phone has a dedicated ambient-temperature sensor, HeatoMeter identifies it separately; most phones do not include one.

Can it prove which app caused overheating?+

No. Foreground activity near a heat reading is timing correlation, not proof that an app caused heat or exact per-app battery consumption.

How do subscriptions work?+

Premium is optional and offered through Google Play with monthly and annual plans. Google Play displays the purchaser’s billing currency, applicable taxes, renewal terms, and cancellation controls.
